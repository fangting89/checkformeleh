# Background of the EASE programme

The EASE (Enhancement for Active Seniors) programme was launched in July 2012 to enhance the safety and comfort of seniors living in HDB flats. Homeowners can choose from a range of senior-friendly fittings to help elderly family members move around their flats safely and more easily.

The programme has been continually reviewed and enhanced. In December 2018, HDB expanded EASE to include portable and customised ramps for residents in older flats with multi-step entrances. In April 2024, HDB introduced a wider range of senior-friendly items under EASE 2.0, including rocker switches, Home Fire Alarm Devices, bidet sprays, wall-mounted foldable shower seats, foldable U-profile grab bars, lowering of toilet entrance kerbs, and widening of toilet entrances.

## Subsidy

The Government pays up to 95% of the cost of the EASE improvement items for Singapore Citizen households, depending on flat type. Flat owners can choose any combination of improvements that best meets their needs and pay only for what they select.

## Application

EASE is offered in conjunction with the Home Improvement Programme (HIP), so works for both can be carried out at the same time. Elderly and vulnerable residents who need EASE fittings earlier, or whose blocks don't qualify for HIP, can apply directly to HDB for the EASE (Direct Application) programme through the e-Service on HDB InfoWEB.
