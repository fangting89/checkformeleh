# ComCare

ComCare provides financial assistance and social support to individuals and families who need help with their daily living expenses.

## Types of financial assistance

- **Short-To-Medium-Term Assistance** — temporary support for families and individuals who are unable to work for a period of time (e.g. due to illness, caregiving responsibilities), are looking for a job, or are earning a low income and require assistance to meet their basic living needs.
- **Long-Term Assistance** — long-term support for persons who are permanently unable to work due to old age, illness or disability, have limited or no means of income, and have little or no family support.
- **Interim Assistance** — immediate financial assistance to individuals and families who require urgent and temporary financial relief.
- Student Care Fee Assistance, Kindergarten Fee Assistance Scheme, and Infant and Childcare Subsidy Scheme are also available for families with children.

## FAQs

**Can we apply for ComCare assistance if our household income is higher than the stated criteria?**
The income criteria for ComCare schemes are not hard thresholds. Social Service Offices (SSOs) assess applicants' circumstances and needs holistically, provide financial assistance, and connect them to community support as needed.

**How long is ComCare assistance given for?**
ComCare Long-Term Assistance is for persons permanently unable to work and with little or no financial means or family support. Short-to-Medium-Term Assistance is typically granted for 3 to 6 months in the first instance, and renewed if the household continues to require support.

**Does the applicant have to be a Singapore Citizen to be eligible?**
The applicant must be a Singapore Citizen or Permanent Resident (where at least one immediate family member of the same household is a Singapore Citizen).
