# Merdeka Generation Package

To honour and thank Merdeka Generation seniors for their contributions to Singapore's development. Supports Merdeka Generation seniors in their silver years and provides peace of mind on healthcare costs.

## Who is eligible

Singapore citizens who:
- Were born from 1 January 1950 to 31 December 1959, and became a Singapore citizen on or before 31 December 1996;

OR

- Were born on or before 31 December 1949, became a Singapore citizen on or before 31 December 1996, and did not receive the Pioneer Generation Package.

Those eligible would have received a notification letter in May 2019. Eligibility can be checked via the LifeSG app or Singpass Mobile App (view your MG e-card).

## Benefits

**Benefit #1: Additional Outpatient Care Subsidies.** More subsidies at Polyclinics, Public SOCs, and CHAS GP/dental clinics. MG Seniors receive an additional 25% off their remaining bill for subsidised services and medications at polyclinics and public SOCs. At CHAS clinics: up to $23.50 subsidy per visit for common illnesses, up to $85 per visit (capped $340/year) for a simple chronic condition, up to $130 per visit (capped $520/year) for complex chronic conditions, plus dental subsidies. Healthier SG screening: $2 fixed fee for Merdeka Generation seniors not enrolled under Healthier SG.

**Benefit #2: CareShield Life Additional Participation Incentives.** Participation incentives of $1,875 ($187.50/year over 10 years) plus additional $1,125 ($112.50/year) if joining and covered under CareShield Life by 31 December 2024. This is in addition to any means-tested premium subsidies MG seniors may receive.

**Benefit #3: Additional MediShield Life Premium Subsidies.** From 1 July 2019, Merdeka Generation seniors receive additional Merdeka Generation Subsidies of 5% of their annual MediShield Life premiums, increasing to 10% from age 76 and above, regardless of household monthly income or Annual Value of home.

## How to apply

No separate application needed for those already eligible — check eligibility via LifeSG or Singpass Mobile App. Keep your NRIC address updated with ICA. Questions: AskMOH.
