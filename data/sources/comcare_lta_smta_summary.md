# Summary of ComCare LTA and SMTA Schemes

Official comparison of ComCare's two main assistance schemes.

## Long-Term Assistance (LTA)

**Target group:** Provides support to those permanently unable to work due to old age, illness or disability, with little or no means of income and family support.

**Eligibility criteria:**
- Singapore Citizen (SC) or Permanent Resident (PR).
- Unable to work due to old age, illness or disability.
- Little or no family support, savings or assets to rely on for basic living expenses. This includes:
  - Elderly persons who receive only small monthly payouts (lower than prevailing LTA rates) from other sources such as CPF Retirement Sum/CPF LIFE, Pension, ElderShield, and the Lease Buyback Scheme.
  - Elderly persons whose children are themselves lower-income (household income of $1,900/month or less, or per capita income of $650/month or less) and unable to support their parents.

**Assistance rendered:**
- Monthly cash assistance for living expenses and household bills (including rental, utilities, and Service & Conservancy Charges).
- Secondary tier assistance for recurring essentials such as medical and hygiene consumables.
- Discretionary tier assistance for one-off purchases of essentials such as healthcare or household appliances.
- Assistance with medical bills at public healthcare institutions (all polyclinics, restructured hospitals, specialist centres).
- Free or highly subsidised access to social support services including home help services and Senior Activity Centres. LTA beneficiaries may also receive supplementary assistance in cash or in kind from community-based agencies (e.g. food rations and vouchers).

## Short-To-Medium-Term Assistance (SMTA)

**Target group:** Provides temporary financial support to lower-income individuals and families temporarily unable to work, looking for a job, or earning a low income and requiring assistance for basic living expenses.

**Eligibility criteria:**
- Singapore Citizen (SC) or Permanent Resident (PR). If the applicant is a PR, at least one immediate family member must be an SC.
- Household income of $1,900 per month or less, or per capita income of $650 per month or less.
- The income eligibility criterion is not a hard threshold — Social Service Offices (SSOs) assess the needs of individuals and families holistically, and exercise flexibility to provide necessary financial assistance and community support.

**Assistance rendered:**
- Monthly cash assistance for living expenses.
- Assistance with household bills (including rental, utilities, and S&CC).
- Assistance with medical bills at public healthcare institutions.
- Employment Assistance such as job search and/or training, provided via Workforce Singapore and the NTUC Employment and Employability Institute.
- Referrals to other government agencies and community partners (e.g. Family Service Centres for casework and counselling).
