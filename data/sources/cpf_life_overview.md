# Receive lifelong monthly payouts with CPF LIFE

CPF Lifelong Income For the Elderly (CPF LIFE) is a national longevity insurance annuity scheme that provides monthly payouts no matter how long you live.

## Why CPF LIFE

Nobody can predict how long anyone will actually live. With CPF LIFE, you can continue receiving a retirement income no matter what age you live to.

## Benefits

- **Payouts for life.** Get a monthly income no matter how long you live. Most private retirement income plans only provide payouts for a limited period.
- **Safe insurance product with risk-free interest rates.** CPF LIFE savings are guaranteed by the Singapore Government. Earn risk-free interest rates of up to 6% per annum (based on the interest rate floor of 4% per annum).
- **More affordable than private annuity plans.** Being non-profit and administered by the CPF Board, CPF LIFE does not incur costs from advertising and agents' commissions.
- CPF LIFE is an insurance product, not an investment product — it protects against the risk of outliving your savings.

## Inclusion

- **Automatic inclusion**: you're automatically included in CPF LIFE if you're a Singapore Citizen or Permanent Resident, born in 1958 or after, and have at least $60,000 in your retirement savings when you start your monthly payouts.
- **Voluntary inclusion**: if not automatically included, you can still enrol any time from age 65 to one month before you turn 80.
- **Opting out**: you can be exempted from CPF LIFE if you have a pension or private annuity plan that pays the same or higher monthly payouts.

## The three CPF LIFE plans

- **Escalating Plan** — monthly payouts start lower initially but grow by 2% a year for life. Suitable if you're worried about rising costs.
- **Standard Plan** — steady monthly payouts that do not increase over time, so does not protect against inflation.
- **Basic Plan** — monthly payouts start low and fall when your CPF balances fall below $60,000.

All three plans provide monthly payouts for life. When you pass away, your CPF LIFE premium balance (if any), together with any remaining CPF savings, will be distributed to your loved ones.

## Deferring payouts

If you have already started receiving payouts but don't need the funds yet, you can defer until age 70. For each year you defer, your payouts increase by up to 7%. Deferring until age 70 increases payouts by up to 35%.
