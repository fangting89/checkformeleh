# Community Health Assist Scheme (CHAS)

CHAS enables all Singapore Citizens, including Pioneer Generation (PG) and Merdeka Generation (MG) cardholders, to receive subsidies for preventive, medical and/or dental care at participating GP and dental clinics.

## Who is eligible

All Singapore Citizens, regardless of income, are eligible for CHAS. Benefits are tiered by household monthly income per person, or Annual Value (AV) of home for households with no income (effective 1 Jan 2025):

| CHAS card | Household monthly income per person | Annual Value of home (no-income households) |
|---|---|---|
| CHAS Green | Above $2,300 | Above $31,000 |
| CHAS Orange | $1,501 – $2,300 | $21,001 – $31,000 |
| CHAS Blue | $1,500 and below | $21,000 and below |

Merdeka Generation and Pioneer Generation cardholders get their own, higher subsidy tier regardless of the above.

## Medical subsidies (per visit)

| Condition | CHAS Green | CHAS Orange | CHAS Blue | Merdeka Generation | Pioneer Generation |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Common illness (e.g. cough, cold) | Not applicable | Up to $10 | Up to $18.50 | Up to $23.50 | Up to $28.50 |
| Simple chronic condition | Up to $28 (capped $112/yr) | $50 (capped $200/yr) | Up to $80 (capped $320/yr) | Up to $85 (capped $340/yr) | Up to $90 (capped $360/yr) |
| Complex chronic conditions | Up to $40 (capped $160/yr) | Up to $80 (capped $320/yr) | Up to $125 (capped $500/yr) | Up to $130 (capped $520/yr) | Up to $135 (capped $540/yr) |

Common illness subsidies are capped at 24 visits per patient per calendar year across all CHAS clinics.

## Dental subsidies

CHAS also subsidises a range of preventive and restorative dental procedures (consultations, extractions, fillings, dentures, crowns, root canal treatment, polishing, scaling, X-rays) at participating clinics, with subsidy amounts increasing by card tier (CHAS Orange < CHAS Blue < Merdeka Generation < Pioneer Generation). From 1 October 2025, subsidies for ten preventive dental procedures were extended to CHAS Orange cardholders, and subsidy limits were raised for seven restorative procedures for PG/MG/Blue/Orange cardholders. Exact amounts vary by procedure — MOH publishes fee benchmarks as a price reference.

## Other benefits

- Eligible Healthier SG enrollees receive enhanced subsidies for Healthier SG screening tests, vaccinations, and selected chronic medications at their enrolled Healthier SG clinic.
- Subsidised referrals from CHAS GPs to Specialist Outpatient Clinics at Public Hospitals, NUCOHS, and National Dental Centre when required.
