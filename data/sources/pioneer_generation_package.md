# Pioneer Generation Package

To honour and thank Pioneers for their hard work and dedication in the early years of Singapore's nation-building. Includes initiatives to provide Pioneers affordable healthcare for life.

## Who is eligible

Singapore citizens who:
- Were born on or before 31 December 1949, and
- Became a Singapore citizen on or before 31 December 1986.

Eligibility can be checked via the LifeSG app or Singpass Mobile App (view your Pioneer Generation e-card). Those eligible would have received a notification letter in June 2014.

## Benefits

**Benefit #1: MediSave Top-Ups.** All Pioneers receive annual MediSave top-ups for life, ranging from $300–$1,200 depending on birth cohort (2026 onward): born 1934 and earlier — $1,200; born 1935–1939 — $700; born 1940–1944 — $500; born 1945–1949 — $300. These top-ups can offset premiums for MediShield Life, ElderShield, and CareShield Life.

**Benefit #2: Additional Outpatient Care Subsidies.** More subsidies at Polyclinics, Public Specialist Outpatient Clinics (Public SOCs), and CHAS clinics. Pioneers receive an additional 50% off their remaining bill for subsidised services and medications at polyclinics and public SOCs. At CHAS clinics: up to $28.50 subsidy per visit for common illnesses, up to $90 per visit (capped $360/year) for a simple chronic condition, up to $135 per visit (capped $540/year) for complex chronic conditions, plus dental subsidies.

**Benefit #3: CareShield Life Additional Participation Incentives.** Pioneers receive participation incentives of $1,875 ($187.50/year over 10 years) plus additional participation incentives of $1,125 ($112.50/year) if they join and are covered under CareShield Life by 31 December 2024.

**Benefit #4: Special MediShield Life Premium Subsidies.** Pioneers receive special Pioneer Generation Subsidies of between 40–60% depending on age, regardless of household monthly income or Annual Value of home.

**Benefit #5: Pioneer Generation Disability Assistance Scheme.** For Pioneers who permanently need assistance in at least three Activities of Daily Living (feeding, washing, dressing, transferring, toileting, walking/moving around): life-long cash assistance of $100 monthly to help with care expenses. More information via the AIC website.

## How to apply

No separate application needed for those already eligible — check eligibility via LifeSG or Singpass Mobile App. Questions: AskMOH.
