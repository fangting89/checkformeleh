# CPF LIFE premiums: how does it work?

An annuity scheme is a contract between you and an insurance company, where the insurer is required to make steady payments to you in the future over a period of time.

## What are CPF LIFE premiums?

The CPF LIFE premium is the amount you pay for the CPF LIFE policy in exchange for regular monthly payouts for as long as you live. The amount varies depending on the CPF LIFE plan you choose.

- If you join the **Escalating** or **Standard** Plan, the savings in your Retirement Account (RA) will be deducted as the annuity premium when you start your payouts. You then receive monthly payouts from your payout start age for as long as you live. Once your CPF LIFE premium is depleted, you continue to receive monthly payouts from the pooled interest that has been accumulated, no matter how long you live.
- If you join the **Basic** Plan, about 10 to 20% of your savings in your RA will be deducted as the annuity premium when you start your payouts. You then receive monthly payouts from the remaining 80–90% of your savings in the RA from your chosen payout start age until one month before you reach 90 years old. After reaching 90, you receive the monthly payout from your CPF LIFE premiums. Because less is used for the premium, the Basic Plan's monthly payout is lower compared to the Standard Plan.

## What CPF LIFE can do for you

CPF LIFE is an annuity designed to provide lifelong monthly income for retirement, helping defray recurring costs like healthcare insurance premiums, utility bills, and grocery bills.
